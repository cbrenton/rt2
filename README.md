A Rust raytracer capable of rendering videos. Working towards this being a [wavefront renderer](https://jacco.ompf2.com/2019/07/18/wavefront-path-tracing/).

My goals with this project were to shore up my abilities to do math and to learn difficult things on my own. As such, this project was written entirely without the assistance of LLMs, sometimes to my detriment. It was also largely written without referring to anyone's sample code, with the exception of camera ray calculation (which was math that I found both annoying and not something that would further my goals) and barycentric coordinates (which is math that I understand relatively well but is past my current ability to derive on my own). For those I used Peter Shirley's amazing [Raytracing In One Weekend](https://raytracing.github.io/books/RayTracingInOneWeekend.html#rays,asimplecamera,andbackground/sendingraysintothescene) as a reference. Everything else was drawn up by me on a whiteboard, over and over again until I could explain it to someone else with no graphics background.

Sometimes projects have real world impact. Sometimes the journey itself is the destination. This project is the latter.
